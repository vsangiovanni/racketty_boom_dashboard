Frequently asked questions

Straight answers — aligned with what we publish for new clients.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Racketty Boom Construction offers free estimates for construction and remodeling projects. We evaluate scope and provide a clear estimate so you can decide without upfront cost or obligation.

How are you different from other contractors?

We emphasize customer service, personal attention, and practical design solutions. Clear communication, reliable scheduling, and quality workmanship are non-negotiable — we aim to exceed expectations, not just meet a minimum bid.

What services do you offer?

New construction and additions, kitchen and bath remodeling, basement finishing, roofing and siding, general contracting, and a wide range of interior and exterior repairs — see the services grid above for the full list.

Do you offer discounts?

We offer discounts for senior citizens and veterans. Ask when you request a quote so we can apply any eligible savings.
